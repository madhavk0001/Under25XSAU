The Manifesto

Not a club.
A crew.

Under25 is India's largest youth media network — 600+ youth-run handles, flagship Summits, and a community that speaks for itself instead of being spoken for. At SAU, that means a chapter run entirely by students, for students from across South Asia sharing one campus.

We run the events, shoot the content, and turn ordinary semesters into the kind of thing people actually remember. If you've got an idea, a camera, a mic, or just energy — there's a seat for you.
